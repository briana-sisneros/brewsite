All beers belong to one of two main families based on how they ferment: ales or lagers.


Ale is a type of beer and these are some types of Ale:

Pale Ale: A balanced, refreshing beer with mild bitterness and citrus hop aromas.

India Pale Ale (IPA): A bold style featuring strong hop flavors, pine or citrus notes, and high bitterness.

Stout and Porter: Dark, rich beers with chocolate, light roast, coffee, and cocoa flavors.

Wheat Beer: A light, cloudy beer with soft textures and notes of banana, clove, or citrus.

Sour Beer: An adventurous style with a tart, acidic, or fruity punch.

Belgian Ale: A complex, aromatic brew with spicy and fruity notes.



Lager is a type of beer and these are some types of Lager:

Pilsner: A bright, light-colored lager with a crisp, herbal hop profile and dry finish.

American Lager: A light, neutral, and highly crisp style.

Dark Lager / Dunkel: A dark beer that remains smooth, offering caramel, toast, and chocolate notes.

Vienna Lager / Amber Lager: An amber-colored, balanced beer with toasted malt sweetness.



Some hybrid styles cross boundaries or use unique ingredients:

Kölsch: A warm-fermented ale that is cold-conditioned like a lager for a crisp, clean taste.

Fruit Beer: Ales or lagers brewed with real fruit for a sweet or tart profile.

Barrel-Aged Beer: Beers aged in oak barrels to pick up rich vanilla and wood flavors.
